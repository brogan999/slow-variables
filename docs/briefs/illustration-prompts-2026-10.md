# Illustration prompts for Parts 44 and 46 (6 Oct 2026)

Sixteen scenes for the owner to generate in the Futures house style. Each becomes one 720 px WebP named by its stem, dropped beside the Futures images and recorded with its prompt and hash by `scripts/futures_images.py`, as the Futures set was. On the page each carries the credit "Illustration made with AI image generation; not evidence". The scenes for "next" and "later" picture this site's judgement, not anything observed.

Append this style block to every subject brief:

> Use case: stylized-concept. A cinematic photograph of a specific imagined scene, 3:2 landscape, 1536x1024. Warm, optimistic, lived-in science fiction is the house style, matching the Futures set. Technology is visibly extraordinary yet integrated with daily life. Favor ivory ceramic, brushed metal, glass, stone, timber, linen and planting. Rich natural color, soft gold highlights, gentle cool shadows, restrained practical illumination. Layered foreground, middle distance and background, believable photographic texture. No text, letters, digits, logos, watermark, recognizable people or characters, real company names or uniforms, named-artist copying or gore.
